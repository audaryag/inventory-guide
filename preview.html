Numbers are made up. Layout, wording, fonts and colours are exactly what the theme and the build steps produce — a 1280 × 720 canvas. Overview is the rebuilt one: the green panel on the left is fixed to the latest month and ignores every slicer, the chart and the table below it show five months at a time, and the two donuts label their slices in per cent. Summary is the rebuilt reconciliation: Inventory (TB), Inventory (MB5B) and Difference as three master columns, plants opening into RM / FG / Consumables, and the across-all-plants block underneath. FG is rebuilt the same way, with MW, Rs Cr. and Days as the three master columns — by plant, then by technology — and the charts below pinned to the latest month, because stock cannot be added across months. RM is the old RM sheet rebuilt the same way: plants, then Module and Cell opening into their materials, with Rs Cr. and Days as the two master columns, and the two plant charts underneath — rupees and days, three plant bars inside each month. Try it: on FG, click a bar — it opens the Detail page for that technology. In Power BI that is right-click → Drill through.
